    • SONGS OF SORROW

    In Songs of Sorrow, rhythm, bullet-hell, and narrative intertwine to deliver an intense and immersive experience. Join Nika on her journey to uncover how she ended up in a mysterious rock pub, run by the even more mysterious Barman.

    Songs Of Sorrow is a 3D video game developed by a team of 12 members from the University of Coruña in 2025, within a timeframe of 4 months, marking our first real experience with the Unreal Engine 5.

    This was my first "professional" project with a large team. My role was Lead Programmer, where, in addition to leading and supervising the programming team, I was responsible for implementing all interface programming, some core mechanics, optimization and version management of the game, and fixing bugs as they arose.

    EXPERIENCE ACQUIRED

    • Ability to lead a video game programming team.
    • Development of an Interactive Dialogue System.
    • Creation of a "Combat" System synchronized with the music.
    • Development of a Translation System.
    • Knowledge about optimization and refactoring of code.
    • Advanced Configuration System.
    • Experience with 3D Projects.
    • Integration of Mini-games.
