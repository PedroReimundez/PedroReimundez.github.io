    • ESCAMPADOR

    The truth is that it's not stopping raining, in fact it's raining A LOT and today is the day the game you've been waiting for so long is released, the latest entry in your favorite saga: Final Fantasix.

    There's nothing left for you to do but go out and get soaked, you don't have a car and your umbrella is broken, equipped with the traditional raincoat (water suit) of your grandfather, you go out into the streets with a goal in mind: if the rain doesn't clear up by itself, you'll have to clear it up yourself...

    TURN YOURSELF INTO THE ESCAMPADOR.

    Escampador is a 2D video game project developed in Godot by 2 people over 2 days.

    This project is part of a Game Jam called "CoruJam II", where we had a weekend to make a game with the main theme "Nunca choveu que non escampara".

    EXPERIENCE ACQUIRED

    • Development of an Interactive Dialog System in the Visual Novel style
    • Creation of a turn-based combat system with enhancements
    • Experience with 2D Projects.
