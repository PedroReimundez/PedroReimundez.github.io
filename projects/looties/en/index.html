    • THE LOOTIES

    The Looties are small harmless creatures that live inside slot machines, they are very shy and rarely let themselves be seen by humans, they have all kinds of forms, sizes, colors… and, although their biology is a mystery, they all hatch from eggs.

    Here is where you come in, a caretaker to whom has been assigned the task of caring for some of the few lootie eggs found in an old gambling hall that collapsed due to new regulations that prevent them from opening near schools and institutes…

    The Looties is a 2D video game project developed in Godot over the course of a month and a half by a team of 4 people, where you take a quiz to obtain a pet to care for while completing a series of mini-games.

    Later on during development, a colleague and I offered to cover the lack of programmers in the project, as the rest were not very familiar with using game engines.

    I participated in this project for only 2 days, when all the art and design of the game's logic was already designed.

    In these 2 days I did all the interface programming for the game, as well as developing the slot machine and question-and-answer system from scratch based on what was envisioned by the design department.
