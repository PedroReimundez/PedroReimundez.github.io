    • MUSBOUND

    Search for a better life, far from the yoke of humans, a caravan of small animals gets trapped in a mysterious cave. Confused but determined to survive, they must collaborate to stay alive in this hostile environment, battered by a terrible disease that turns those it infects into beasts.

    Put yourself in the skin of Cartucho the mouse and explore the cave, collect resources and manage your camp to try to survive another day. And remember, time is pressing.

    Musbound is a playable experience that works as a prototype for a (possible) future homonymous video game. It is the fruit of the study and development of an effective and adaptable methodology, through a substantial documentation available on the website of Crunia.
