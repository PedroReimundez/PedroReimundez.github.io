    • MY CUTIE BABY ALIEN

    In My Cutie Baby Alien From Space Demands Cute Clothes Or Else... (Shitty Job Simulator) The Game! you will control Josema, a twenty-year-old unemployed man who, by a twist of fate, encounters an "adorable" alien who begins demanding nice clothes in exchange for staying alive.

    To achieve this, Josema must find work in the hospitality industry. Start working at a fast-food restaurant to earn money and buy clothes for the alien.
    The goal of the game is to go to the restaurant, earn money by completing orders, and after each turn, return home and buy nice clothes for your alien baby so it doesn't get angry.

    This was the first time I worked as a programmer, besides being my first video game.

    It was a project that only lasted 1 month, but later some members decided to revive the project.
