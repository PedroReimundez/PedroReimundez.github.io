    FINAL FANTASIX

    In Final Fantasy IX, you take on the role of a hero named Fahn, who is in the final stage of his RPG adventure and must face the game's final boss.

    But after defeating him, he realizes the end credits are already rolling and he hasn't yet managed to get the attention of the princess kidnapped by the villain. Therefore, you'll have to try to win her over before the credits finish.

    Final Fantasix is a 2D videogame project developed in Godot Engine in less than a week by a team of six people.

    This project was part of a Game Jam called "Clubu Jam", where we had one week to create a game whose main theme was "delaying the inevitable."

    EXPERIENCE ACQUIRED

    • Development of an original system based on time limits.
    • Development of a Visual Novel-style Dialogue System.
    • Experience with 2D Projects.
