    • Trick or Mistreat

    It’s Halloween, which means the long-awaited annual tradition of going to other people’s homes to ask for treats (food) has finally arrived, all without anyone giving you strange looks or calling the police. 

    Someone knocks at your door, and bag of candy at the ready, you head over to open it. 
    But this time, what’s waiting outside isn’t a group of people in costumes; it’s monsters disguised as humans. 
    They don’t want candy, they want something else and they’re threatening to get rid of you if you don’t hand it over...

    Trick or Mistreat? 
    That is the law of the monsters.
