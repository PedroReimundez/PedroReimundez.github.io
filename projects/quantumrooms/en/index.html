    • The QuantumRooms

    Quantonio is a researcher dedicated to discovering and unraveling the secrets of quantum computing. Believing he is smarter than anyone else, he builds the definitive quantum computer with his own hands, the Qmión (approximately 32³² qubits). But... when he turns it on, something goes wrong and he is transported inside the supercomputer, in this new world governed by quantum laws, he must adapt, explore and survive if he wants to find the way out to the "classical world".

    The Quantumrooms is a 2D video game developed in Godot over the course of a week by a team of 4 people.

    This project is part of a Game Jam called "BrinQo Quantum Jam", with the theme of Quantum Computing.

    In this project I did all the game programming and developed several of the rooms that can be seen in the game.
